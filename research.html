Research

Reduced density matrices, correlation, and relativistic effects.

Areas of specialization

Reduced density matrix functional theory

Natural orbital functionals and pair coupled-cluster theory, from model systems to molecules. I proposed relativistic RDMFT (SciPost Chem. 2022) and have studied its time-reversal symmetry with complex-valued orbitals.

Many-body perturbation theory

GW and RPA methods: linearized GW density matrices and total energies for molecules and solids, and their coupling with natural orbital functional theory.

Electron correlation and pair densities

Correlation indicators, the Coulomb hole, and intracule and extracule densities, studied in atoms, bonds and model systems such as the Hubbard model and Hooke atoms.

Relativistic quantum chemistry

Relativistic RDMFT, which I proposed, for compounds of heavy elements (see the ReReDMFT project), and relativistic effects on pair densities.

Optical properties and excited states

Nonlinear optical properties with RPA and double-hybrid functionals, orbital partitioning of optical properties, and excitonic models with environmental embedding from GW/BSE.

Density functional theory

Double-hybrid and range-separated functionals, and conceptual DFT.

Machine learning in quantum chemistry

Learning correlation energies, such as MP2, for organic compounds.

Projects

My funded projects are described under Projects, including MC2021/22 ReReDMFT, which extends reduced density matrix functional theory to compounds of heavy elements.
